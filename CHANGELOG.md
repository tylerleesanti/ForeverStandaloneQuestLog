# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Standalone two-pane quest log window in the Classic quest log art, opened with L at the classic spot on the left side of the screen.
- Quest levels, difficulty colours, and Elite / Dungeon / Daily / Weekly / Repeatable / Complete / Failed tags, with a countdown on timed quests.
- Search by quest or zone, collapsible zones, and an All button to expand or collapse them all.
- Reopens on the quest you last had selected (or the one you're super-tracking).
- Details page with objectives, description, and rewards: XP, money, and the reward items with their tooltips (Shift to compare, Shift-click to link, Ctrl-click to try on).
- With Questie: reputation rewards, a chain icon in the list for quests that are part of a chain, and an info icon after the quest's name that shows who gives and takes the quest and where, its required level, and the quests before and after it in its chain.
- Hover a quest in the list for its objectives and progress; finished quests say where to turn them in, in the tooltip and on the details page.
- A button to use a quest's item ("Use the Torch on the Hut") in the bottom bar, out of combat.
- Party members on each quest: a count in the list and their names on the details page.
- Abandon, Share and Track buttons; shift-click to link, ctrl-click to track.
- Show Map button: opens the world map where the quest is turned in (completed quests) or where its next objective is, using Questie's data when Questie is installed.
- Clicking a quest in Questie's or Blizzard's tracker (including "View in Quest Log") or a quest timer opens it in this window, and the Quest Log micro button opens it like L.
- Settings panel (cogwheel): text sizes, category title colour, window width and height, a shadow that makes the quest steps easier to read, and feature toggles.
- Padlock button to lock the window's position; right-click it to reset the position.
